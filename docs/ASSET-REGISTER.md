Research conclusion: no free, clearly licensed candidate found here combines realistic close-up anatomy, textured forearms, and a ready GLB/FBX export. WRAD ARMS and DevMops are the most practical CC0 downloads but are deliberately low-poly; the Lieker model is the best anatomical reference but is hand-only and CC BY-NC. The current authored rig should remain until a custom or otherwise commercially safe replacement passes the full grip review.
