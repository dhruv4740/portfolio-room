# Full audit — 2026-09-23 (Opus 5.5)

Read-only pass. No repo code changed, nothing committed. Evidence was gathered
in the in-app browser (AMD Radeon iGPU, ANGLE D3D11), with a production build
and with Playwright on the real GPU.

## Verdict

The product is further along than the mood around it suggests. The launchpad,
the dhruvOS handoff, four real project UIs running in windows, the content
writing (the `decision`/`broke` fields are genuinely strong) and the /work
fallback all work. Three things hold it back:

1. **The hands at HEAD are a regression, not a ceiling.** Earlier builds had
   clearly separated fingers (see below).
2. **It is not shipped.** There is no remote, no host and no share image, and
   the meta description currently makes a false claim.
3. **Process debt is costing more than code debt.** The project has 24
   `PROMPT-*` docs and 17 review docs, and its ownership rules contradict
   each other. Every new session spends its budget re-reading who owns what.

## 1. Hands — measured, with a fix path

`scripts/shots/hands/hands-comparison.png` shows the same seated rest view,
built and rendered on the real GPU at six commits. Each frame is the whole app
as it stood at that commit, not a swapped mesh.

| | Commit | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| A | `8ee163b` 09-17 | Jointed rig. Stylised, clay-like, fingers readable |
| B | `504189c` 09-20 01:14 | First continuous skin. Fingers splayed and separate |
| C | `fce4507` 09-20 03:22 | Flatter proportions. Separate fingers |
| D | `aad580e` 09-20 04:59 | Assets and winter studio arrive. Hands identical to E |
| E | `dd85216` 09-20 11:25 | Last commit before the fusion. Separate fingers |
| F | HEAD | Fingers fused, and both hands meet and merge at the thumbs |

- **When it broke.** Assets arriving (D) did not change the hands. They broke
  at `6a13047`, which raised the smooth-union blend radius from 3 mm to
  6/12 mm, and at `11a2bce`, which added the torso shift and the keyboard
  support changes. That matches Opus's earlier diagnosis in
  `OPUS-HANDS-AND-REACH.md`, and these frames confirm it independently.
- **Why "dead".** This part is inferred, not measured. Every version is a
  single flat-albedo standard material. There is no subsurface-like
  falloff, no nails, no creases and no colour variation, so skin reads as
  clay under the warm lamp. The fix is material work: wrap/SSS
  approximation, a subtle albedo/roughness map, nails and knuckle creases.
  Geometry alone won't do it.
- **Fast path.** Bring back the `dd85216`-era blend radii, rebake
  `hands-v1.bin(.gz)`, and clamp the torso offset's vertical component. That
  restores E-quality hands in HEAD's room. Then do the material pass.
- **Presentation option.** Games routinely hide or lower idle first-person
  hands. Hands could appear only while typing, reaching or holding. That cuts
  their screen time at rest, which is where a visitor stares longest.

The checkpoint worktrees are still available at
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Temp\handcmp\<sha>`. Their previews are running on
`localhost:4201`–`4205` (A–E) for live comparison. Kill them when done.

## 3. Performance and payload

- **JS (prod, gzip):** Scan 106 KB, three 163 KB, React 60 KB. Reasonable.
  PhoneScreen and both phone apps are bundled into Scan eagerly, although
  most visitors never pick up the phone.
- **Models and audio, measured over the network on a cold load:** about
  12.6 MB. That breaks down as winter-city.glb 4.5 MB, desk-lamp.glb 2.9 MB
  (for a lamp), song.mp3 1.8 MB, hands-v1.bin.gz 1.6 MB, ceiling fixture
  0.8 MB, mouse 0.6 MB and keyboard 0.4 MB.
- **Textures:** roughly another 7–8 MB of 1k JPG PBR maps, estimated from
  file sizes. These were served from cache in this run, so they were not
  measured over the network.
- **Loaded on the standing landing with no need there:** song.mp3 (only
  used when the headphones go on) and the hand binary (only used when
  seated).
- **Biggest wins:**
  - glTF meshopt/Draco compression plus KTX2 (Basis) textures, probably
    cutting the ~20 MB to a few MB (an estimate, not measured);
  - lazy-loading the song, the hands and the phone apps.
- **GPU cost:** no new measurements this session. The prior documented
  numbers were 9.6 / 14.6 / 22 ms median seated at 0.8× / 1× / 1.25× pixel
  ratio, on this iGPU.

## 4. Architecture and codebase

- `WorkspaceStage.jsx` is a single ~1,100-line `useEffect` closure holding the
  renderer, orbit controls, raycasting, markers, flights, the phone, the
  notebook, the hands, audio hooks and stats. 22 lines exceed 200 characters,
  with minified-style one-liners. `render()` allocates new `Vector3`s every
  frame (line 227). This is the main maintainability risk, because every
  agent edits the same closure. A split along existing seams would help:
  - interaction/raycast;
  - held-object controllers (phone, notebook, pen, coffee, award,
    résumé);
  - hand driving;
  - render loop plus stats.

  This is a refactor, and I'd only do it after the hands are fixed and on
  request.
- `PhoneScreen.jsx` has 2,822 lines. **Not read this session.**
- Coordination between Scan and the stage runs through `window` CustomEvents
  (`workspace:coffee|foley|headphones`) and globals (`__v3Music`,
  `__perfGovernor`, `__cityAmbience`, `__v3test`, `__phoneTimeScale`). That
  works, but it is an implicit API.
- **Dead code still tracked**, contradicting "Repo cleaned":
  - `src/game/`, `src/hooks/`, `src/store/`, `src/data/`, `src/lib/`,
    `src/lab/`, `src/content/`, `src/shaders/` and `src/index.css`;
  - empty `src/components/*` and `src/flagship/`.

  The live graph is 71 modules, all from `src/v3`.
- **Unused dependencies:**
  - `framer-motion`, `lenis` and `r3f-perf` have no imports anywhere;
  - `zustand` and `howler` are imported only by dead `src/store` and
    `src/hooks`;
  - `public/os/index.html` loads howler from a CDN (the live embed uses
    `embed.html`).
- **Stale comments** still describe the splat renderer: `App.jsx:5`,
  `capability.js:60`, `vite.config.js` (Spark) and the `splatCount` key in
  `onReady`.
- **Two room builders coexist:** the live `buildWorkspace.js` and the
  unadopted `buildCinematicWorkspace.js` plus `cinematicLayout.js` and
  `cinematicRoomInteractions.js`, about 1,450 lines of parallel scene code.
  Adopt the candidate or delete it; carrying both indefinitely is the
  expensive option.

## 6. Creative direction, ranked by visitor reach × cost

1. **Ship it.** Cloudflare Pages or Vercel, static, no rewrites (see bug 3),
   plus a domain. Nothing below matters at zero visitors.
2. **Share card.** Render a composed real-GPU frame of the room as the
   `og:image` and fix the meta copy. Most people meet the site as a link
   preview.
3. **Mobile deck.** Today it is black with a giant "01". Phones are where
   LinkedIn clicks land. Options:
   - put a short pre-rendered loop of the real room (rendered with the
     existing GPU harness) behind the deck;
   - add a "send this to my desktop" action (copy link / email).
4. **The first 10 seconds.** Replace "INITIALISING" with a real progress
   state. Fix the monitor marker overlap. Make the landing frame a composed
   shot. Today the pendant is cropped at the top and the room reads dim and
   muddy.
5. **Lighting before props.** The biggest realism lever is a proper
   lightmap/irradiance bake after the scale fix, plus restrained bloom on the
   monitor and lamp. More licensed props won't move it much. The current
   bake is documented as reflecting an old lamp position.
6. **Fix the scale.** The live room is about 1.6× human scale, which gives
   the "toy keyboard on a giant desk" look. The true-scale candidate exists.
   Adopting it and the hand kinematics is one job, and it is now unblocked
   (single owner).
7. **Surface the writing.** The `decision` and `broke` paragraphs are the
   best recruiter content on the site. Show them beside each running demo
   (a "case file" side panel in the OS window) instead of behind "Details".
8. **A builder's page.** A "how this was built" page, with GPU timer
   numbers, the iframe-on-projected-quad trick and the real-app mock layer.
   Cheap, and aimed squarely at the flex audience.
9. **Hands.** Restore now (section 1). After that, decide between realism
   (material pass, or a real bare CC0 rigged hand) and hands only when
   interacting.
