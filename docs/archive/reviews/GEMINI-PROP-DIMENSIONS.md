## 2. Detailed Prop Specifications & Physical Context

### 2.1 Desk Coffee Mug

*Context*: Standard ceramic/stoneware cylindrical mug designed for hot beverages on office desks. Ceramic requires substantial wall thickness for thermal mass and mechanical shock resistance.

- **Outer diameter at rim**:
  - **Range**: `80 – 86 mm` (3.15″ – 3.39″).
  - **Representative Value**: `82 mm` (3.23″).
  - *Variations*: Narrow diner mugs sit at 80–82 mm; wide latte/cappuccino cups expand to 95–105 mm.
  - *Source*: **Tuxton China Inc.** specifications for Model `BLM-1002` (Diner Mug 10 oz, top rim diameter: 3.25″ = 82.55 mm); **Libbey Foodservice** Ceramic Mug line specifications.
- **Height**:
  - **Range**: `90 – 102 mm` (3.54″ – 4.02″).
  - **Representative Value**: `95 mm` (3.74″).
  - *Source*: Tuxton China specifications (height: 3.75″ = 95.25 mm); **IKEA 365+** mug product sheet (height: 90 mm).
- **Handle projection**:
  - **Range**: `30 – 40 mm` clearance from the outer cylinder wall.
  - **Representative Value**: `35 mm` (giving a total mug width of `82 + 35 = 117 mm`).
  - *Ergonomics*: Interior finger clearance requires 22–26 mm; handle cross-section adds 10–14 mm.
  - *Source*: Libbey Foodservice commercial mug engineering cut sheets (overall width 4.625″ = 117.48 mm against 3.25″ rim diameter).
- **Wall thickness**:
  - **Range**: `4.0 – 6.5 mm` (diner vitrified ceramic: 5.0–7.0 mm; fine bone china: 2.5–3.5 mm).
  - **Representative Value**: `5.0 mm`.
  - *Source*: **ASTM C373-18** (Standard Test Method for Water Absorption, Bulk Density, Apparent Porosity, and Apparent Specific Gravity of Fired Whiteware Products) and commercial hospitality foodservice standards (Syracuse China / Tuxton commercial wall gauge).

### 2.2 Over-Ear Headphones

*Context*: Professional studio monitor headphones (circumaural / closed-back) resting flat or standing on a desk.

- **Earcup outer diameter / span**:
  - **Range**:
    - Circular format (e.g. Beyerdynamic DT 770/880/990): `98 – 104 mm` diameter.
    - Oval format (e.g. Audio-Technica ATH-M50x, Sony MDR-7506): `98 – 108 mm` (height) × `78 – 85 mm` (width).
  - **Representative Value**: `102 mm × 83 mm` (oval studio standard) or `100 mm` (circular).
  - *Source*: **Audio-Technica Corporation** ATH-M50x technical specifications / replacement pad dimensions (`100 mm × 82 mm`); **Beyerdynamic GmbH & Co. KG** DT 770 PRO spare parts catalogue (outer earpad ring diameter: `100 mm`); **Sony Corporation** MDR-7506 Service Manual.
- **Earcup depth (cushion face to outer housing backplate)**:
  - **Range**: `40 – 52 mm` (acoustic chamber housing: 22–28 mm; memory foam/pleather cushion: 18–24 mm).
  - **Representative Value**: `45 mm`.
  - *Source*: Beyerdynamic DT 770 PRO mechanical enclosure drawings (housing depth 24 mm + earpad uncompressed depth 22 mm); Audio-Technica ATH-M50x mechanical specifications.
- **Headband arc outer width**:
  - **Range**: `165 – 195 mm` across the outer faces of the earcups when resting naturally in an unexpanded state.
  - **Representative Value**: `175 mm`.
  - *Ergonomics*: Head simulator standard IEC 60268-7 specifies an ear-to-ear breadth of ~145–150 mm; sprung steel/plastic headbands clamp to ~140 mm width resting, flaring to ~175 mm outside cup housings.
  - *Source*: **IEC 60268-7** (Sound system equipment - Part 7: Headphones and earphones); Audio-Technica ATH-M50x dimensional drawings.
- **Folded footprint**:
  - **Range**:
    - Collapsible inward (yokes hinged into the headband arc, e.g. M50x, MDR-7506): `170 – 190 mm` (length) × `130 – 150 mm` (width) × `75 – 90 mm` (height).
    - Swivel-flat (cups rotated 90° to lay parallel to desk, e.g. Bose QC45, Sony WH-1000XM5): `195 – 215 mm` × `170 – 190 mm` × `45 – 55 mm`.
  - **Representative Value**: `180 mm × 140 mm × 80 mm` (inward collapsed studio monitor posture).
  - *Source*: Audio-Technica ATH-M50x travel pouch dimensions; Sony MDR-7506 Operating Instructions (Compact folding mechanism section).

### 2.3 Desk Photo / Award Frame (Portrait, 6″ × 8″ Print)

*Context*: Portrait orientation tabletop frame holding a standard nominal 6″ × 8″ photograph or award certificate (`152.4 mm × 203.2 mm` aperture).

- **Outer width**:
  - **Range**: `180 – 215 mm` (governed by moulding width).
  - **Representative Value**: `192 mm` (for a standard 20 mm moulding face: `152.4 mm opening + 2 × 20 mm - 0.8 mm rabbet lip ≈ 192 mm`).
  - *Source*: **Larson-Juhl** Tabletop Moulding Collection profiles; **Nielsen Bainbridge** frame profiles.
- **Outer height**:
  - **Range**: `230 – 265 mm`.
  - **Representative Value**: `243 mm` (`203.2 mm opening + 2 × 20 mm ≈ 243 mm`).
  - *Source*: Larson-Juhl moulding specification guides; **Craft Inc.** tabletop photo frame hardware catalog.
- **Frame border width (moulding face width)**:
  - **Range**: `12 – 30 mm` (minimalist modern metal profile: 12–15 mm; bevelled/wood profile: 20–30 mm).
  - **Representative Value**: `20 mm` (~0.79″ / standard 3/4″ stock).
  - *Source*: **PPFA** (Professional Picture Framers Association) standards for tabletop mouldings; Larson-Juhl classic wood profile specifications.
- **Kickstand foot offset from back panel**:
  - **Range**: `50 – 80 mm` horizontal displacement from the frame back to the strut tip resting on the desk.
  - **Representative Value**: `65 mm`.
  - *Geometric Rationale*: Tabletop frames are engineered for a backward lean angle of `14° – 18°` from vertical to prevent forward tipping while minimising overhead ceiling glare. For a 240 mm frame with an easel strut hinged at 60% height (~145 mm), an offset of 65 mm creates an angle $\arcsin(65 / \sqrt{145^2 + 65^2}) \approx 15.5^\circ$.
  - *Source*: **Craft Inc.** Easel Hardware Specifications (Strut Hinges, Self-Supporting Backs Model #4210 series); British Standards Institution (BSI) / ISO guidelines for self-supporting tabletop displays.

### 2.4 Wooden Pencil (Hexagonal)

*Context*: Standard #2 / HB wooden writing and drafting pencil with hexagonal cross-section preventing rolling on desks.

- **Length new**:
  - **Range**: `175 – 192 mm` (plain unsharpened wood-cased: 175–178 mm; ferruled with eraser plug: 188–192 mm).
  - **Representative Value**: `188 mm` (7.40″) for complete pencil with aluminium ferrule and rubber eraser; `175 mm` for unferruled artist pencil.
  - *Source*: **Dixon Ticonderoga Co.** #2 Yellow Pencil product specification sheet (overall length: 7.4″ = 188 mm); **Faber-Castell AG** Castell 9000 specifications (unsharpened total length: 175 mm); **ISO 9177** / **DIN 51172**.
- **Across-flats width (facet-to-opposite-facet)**:
  - **Range**: `6.8 – 7.4 mm`.
  - **Representative Value**: `7.1 mm` across flats (`8.2 mm` across corners/vertices).
  - *Geometric Rationale*: For a regular hexagon, width across corners $D = \frac{W}{\cos(30^\circ)} \approx 1.1547 \times W$. With $W = 7.1\text{ mm}$, $D = 8.2\text{ mm}$.
  - *Source*: **Faber-Castell AG** Castell 9000 engineering data (lead core $\varnothing 2.0\text{ mm}$, pencil diameter across flats: $7.1\text{ mm}$); **KUM GmbH** pencil entry collar gauge (8.0 mm bore fits standard pencils up to 7.8 mm point-to-point).
- **Tip cone length (exposed sharpened wood cone + graphite tip)**:
  - **Range**: `16 – 25 mm`.
    - Standard desktop sharpener (21°–23° included cone angle): `16 – 18 mm`.
    - Long-point draughtsman/artist sharpener (15°–18° included cone angle): `22 – 26 mm`.
  - **Representative Value**: `18 mm` (14 mm wood shoulder + 4 mm exposed graphite lead core).
  - *Source*: **KUM GmbH & Co. KG** sharpener blade geometry specifications (Standard wedge sharpener cut angle: 21°, cone length 17.5 mm; KUM Masterpiece two-step long point cut length: 24.5 mm).

### 2.5 Under-Desk Pedestal Drawer

*Context*: Mobile or fixed under-desk storage unit with castors, box/utility drawers, and a lower hanging file drawer. Designed to roll cleanly beneath standard desk surfaces.

- **Width**:
  - **Range**: `380 – 420 mm` (North American standard 15.0″ = 381 mm; European standard 390–420 mm).
  - **Representative Value**: `390 mm` (15.35″).
  - *Source*: **Steelcase Inc.** Universal Mobile Pedestal Specification Guide (Width: 15″ = 381 mm); **Herman Miller Inc.** Tu Metal Mobile Pedestal (Width: 15″ = 381 mm); **Bisley** Note Pedestal range (Width: 420 mm).
- **Depth**:
  - **Range**: `500 – 600 mm` (compact 500 mm depth for 600 mm desks; deep 570–600 mm for 800 mm desks).
  - **Representative Value**: `540 mm` (21.25″).
  - *Source*: Steelcase TS Series Pedestal (Depth: 22.5″ = 571 mm; compact 19.75″ = 502 mm); Herman Miller Tu Pedestal (Depth: 20″ = 508 mm and 24″ = 610 mm).
- **Height (including castors)**:
  - **Range**: `540 – 680 mm`.
  - **Representative Value**: `600 mm` (23.62″).
  - *Clearance Rationale*: Standard office desk worksurfaces are fixed at 720–740 mm from floor with a 25–40 mm top apron/underside beam. A 600 mm pedestal leaves 60–80 mm clearance for cables and pull-out ease.
  - *Source*: **ANSI/BIFMA X5.9-2019** (American National Standard For Office Furnishings - Storage Units); Steelcase Universal Pedestal with castors (Height: 22.75″ = 578 mm standard); Herman Miller Tu Mobile Pedestal (Height: 21.75″ = 552 mm to 27.25″ = 692 mm).
- **Single drawer front height**:
  - **Range**:
    - Utility / Box drawer: `130 – 165 mm`.
    - Filing drawer (letter / A4): `280 – 330 mm`.
  - **Representative Value**: `145 mm` (Box drawer front); `295 mm` (File drawer front).
  - *Vertical Stack Rationale*: Standard Box/Box/File configuration: $145 + 145 + 295 = 585\text{ mm}$ fascia height + castors ($50\text{ mm}$) - reveals/gaps ($35\text{ mm}$) = $600\text{ mm}$ overall unit height.
  - *Source*: Steelcase Universal Pedestal Specification Sheet (Box front: 5.75″ = 146 mm; File front: 11.5″ = 292 mm).
- **Drawer travel (extension distance from cabinet face)**:
  - **Range**:
    - Box drawer: `300 – 380 mm` (standard 3/4-extension slides, ~75% depth).
    - File drawer: `450 – 520 mm` (full-extension heavy-duty ball-bearing slides, 100% depth).
  - **Representative Value**: `350 mm` (Box drawer, 3/4 extension); `480 mm` (File drawer, full extension).
  - *Source*: **Accuride International Inc.** Technical Data Sheets (Model 3832 Full Extension Slide travel = nominal length 450–500 mm; Model 2132 3/4 Extension Slide travel = 350 mm); **Blum Inc.** drawer runner technical catalogs.
